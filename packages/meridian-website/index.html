FAQ

      
Common questions

      

        

          Is DownAI really free to download?
          
Yes. Download and use the full Monaco editor, tabs, and project explorer at no cost. Chat, terminal, and git require an active Pro subscription.

        

        

          How do I activate Pro after paying?
          
After checkout you receive a license key by email. Open DownAI → Settings → Plan → Activate license, or press Ctrl+Shift+P and search "Activate License".

        

        

          Do I need my own OpenAI API key?
          
Not on Pro — hosted models are included. Free tier is editor-only; if you add a key in Settings later, local usage stays on your device.

        

        

          Can I cancel anytime?
          
Yes. Cancel in Stripe and your license remains valid until the end of the billing period. No long-term contracts on Pro.

        

        

          Is my code sent to your servers?
          
Only when you use Pro chat or hosted AI — messages go through our proxy to generate responses. We don't train models on your code. See our Privacy Policy.

        

        

          Does DownAI support Save As and split editing?
          
Yes — both are included in the free editor. Use Ctrl+Shift+S for Save As and Ctrl+\ for split views.

        

        

          Windows and Mac both supported?
          
Yes. DownAI runs natively on Windows 10/11 and macOS 11+. Apple Silicon and Intel Mac builds are included in the same installer.
